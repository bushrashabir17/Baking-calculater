Basque Burnt Cheesecake

Caramelized top, creamy center, no crust

Home Cakes Cupcakes Cookies Brownies Pizza & Pastry Recipes

About this recipe

Basque burnt cheesecake comes from San Sebastian in Spain and became a social media favorite because it is so easy. There is no crust and no water bath. You mix the batter, bake it at a very high temperature, and let the top turn deep brown. The dark top is meant to look that way, and it gives a slightly caramel flavor. The center stays soft and creamy.

  • Serves: 8
  • Pan: 20 cm (8 inch) round, at least 7 cm deep
  • Prep: 15 min
  • Bake: 35 to 40 min
  • Chill: 4 hours

Ingredients

IngredientGrams / mlCups / spoons
Cream cheese, room temperature600 g2⅔ cups
Sugar150 g¾ cup
Eggs, room temperature3 large3 large
Fresh cream (whipping cream)250 ml1 cup
All-purpose flour (maida)20 g2 tbsp
Vanilla essence5 ml1 tsp
Salt1 ga pinch

Use full-fat block cream cheese. Spreadable tub cheese is too soft and can make the cake runny.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Take the cream cheese and eggs out of the fridge 1 hour ahead.
  2. Line the pan with two overlapping sheets of baking paper, pressing them in so the paper stands about 5 cm above the rim. Crumple the paper first to make it easier to fit.
  3. Beat the cream cheese and sugar until completely smooth, with no lumps.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing on low speed after each.
  5. Mix in the cream, vanilla and salt.
  6. Sift in the flour and mix gently until just combined. Do not beat in air, or the cake will puff up and crack.
  7. Pour into the pan. Tap it on the counter to release big bubbles.
  8. Bake 35 to 40 minutes. The top should be very dark brown and the middle should wobble like jelly when you shake the pan gently.
  9. Cool at room temperature for 1 hour. The cake will sink in the middle. That is normal.
  10. Chill at least 4 hours, then lift out using the paper and slice with a hot knife.

Tips for success

  • Do not open the oven in the first 25 minutes.
  • If your top browns too fast, move the pan to a lower rack for the last 10 minutes.
  • Ovens vary. The center should still wobble when it comes out, because it sets as it cools.
  • It keeps for 4 days in the fridge. The flavor is best on day two.
Why did my cheesecake crack?

Usually from beating too much air into the batter or from baking too long. Mix on low speed and take it out while the center still wobbles.

Is it supposed to be this dark?

Yes. The dark top is the signature of this cake. If it tastes bitter, lower the oven temperature by 10°C next time.
