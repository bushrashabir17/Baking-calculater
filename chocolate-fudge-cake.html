Chocolate Fudge Cake

Moist layers with rich chocolate ganache

Home Cakes Cupcakes Cookies Brownies Pizza & Pastry Recipes

About this recipe

This is a one-bowl chocolate cake made with oil, so it stays moist for days. The batter is thin, and that is correct. Hot water or coffee brings out the chocolate flavor. Finish it with a glossy chocolate ganache for a fudgy bakery-style cake.

  • Serves: 12
  • Pans: two 20 to 23 cm (8 to 9 inch) rounds
  • Prep: 20 min
  • Bake: 30 to 40 min

Ingredients

For the cake

IngredientGrams / mlCups / spoons
Sugar400 g2 cups
All-purpose flour (maida)210 g1¾ cups
Unsweetened cocoa powder65 g¾ cup
Baking powder6 g1½ tsp
Baking soda7 g1½ tsp
Salt6 g1 tsp
Eggs2 large2 large
Milk240 ml1 cup
Vegetable oil120 ml½ cup
Vanilla essence10 ml2 tsp
Boiling water or hot coffee240 ml1 cup

For the chocolate ganache

IngredientAmount
Dark chocolate, chopped250 g
Fresh cream (hot)250 ml (1 cup)
Butter30 g (2 tbsp)

Cups used: flour 120 g per cup, sugar 200 g per cup.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C. Grease two round pans and line the bottoms with baking paper.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the sugar, flour, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
  3. Add the eggs, milk, oil and vanilla. Beat for about 2 minutes until smooth.
  4. Stir in the boiling water or hot coffee. The batter will be thin.
  5. Divide the batter between the two pans.
  6. Bake 30 to 35 minutes for 23 cm pans, or 35 to 40 minutes for 20 cm pans. A toothpick should come out with just a few moist crumbs.
  7. Cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack and cool completely.
  8. Make the ganache: pour the hot cream over the chopped chocolate. Wait 2 minutes, then stir until smooth. Stir in the butter.
  9. Let the ganache cool and thicken for about 30 minutes, stirring now and then.
  10. Spread some ganache between the layers, then cover the top and sides with the rest.

Tips for success

  • Do not worry that the batter is thin. It bakes into a soft, moist crumb.
  • Never frost a warm cake. The ganache will melt and slide off.
  • Use good dark chocolate for the ganache. It sets and tastes better than cheap compound chocolate.
  • Store at room temperature for 2 days, or in the fridge for up to 5 days.
Can I bake this as cupcakes?

Yes. Fill liners two-thirds full and bake at 175°C for 18 to 22 minutes. The recipe makes about 24 cupcakes.

Why did my cake sink in the middle?

Usually from opening the oven too early, old baking powder, or an oven that is too hot. Do not open the door in the first 20 minutes.
