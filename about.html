About Us

Baking Quantity Calculator is a free tool made for new home bakers. Many beginners are not sure how much flour, sugar, butter and eggs they need for a dozen cupcakes, a 1 lb cake, or a bigger celebration cake. This site answers that in seconds.

Our amounts are based on a classic butter sponge recipe and are scaled by weight, so they stay consistent from small cupcakes to large cakes. We show both grams and cups so you can bake with or without a kitchen scale.

Our goal is simple: make baking easier and less wasteful for everyone. If you have a suggestion for a new tool or a correction, please contact us.
