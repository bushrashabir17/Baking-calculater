Fudgy Brownies

Dense, chocolatey and easy

Home Cakes Cupcakes Cookies Brownies Pizza & Pastry Recipes

About this recipe

These brownies are made by melting butter and chocolate together, so they come out dense and fudgy rather than cakey. Beating the eggs well with the sugar gives the shiny, crackly top. You need one bowl and a spoon.

  • Makes: 16 squares
  • Pan: 20 cm (8 inch) square
  • Prep: 15 min
  • Bake: 22 to 28 min

Ingredients

IngredientGrams / mlCups / spoons
Butter115 g½ cup
Dark chocolate, chopped150 gabout 1 cup
Sugar150 g¾ cup
Eggs2 large2 large
Vanilla essence5 ml1 tsp
All-purpose flour (maida)65 g½ cup plus 1 tbsp
Unsweetened cocoa powder20 g3 tbsp
Salt1 g¼ tsp
Chocolate chips or walnuts (optional)60 g⅓ cup

Cups used: flour 120 g per cup, sugar 200 g per cup, butter 227 g per cup.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C. Line a 20 cm square pan with baking paper, leaving extra paper over the sides to lift the brownies out.
  2. Melt the butter and dark chocolate together in a heatproof bowl over simmering water, or in the microwave in short 20-second bursts. Stir until smooth.
  3. Let it cool for 5 minutes. Stir in the sugar.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each. Stir in the vanilla and beat for 1 minute until glossy.
  5. Sift the flour, cocoa and salt over the bowl. Fold in gently until just combined.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips or walnuts if using.
  7. Spread the batter in the pan and bake 22 to 28 minutes. A toothpick should come out with moist crumbs, not wet batter.
  8. Cool completely in the pan, at least 1 hour. This helps them set so they cut cleanly.
  9. Lift out and cut into 16 squares with a sharp knife.

Tips for success

  • Do not overbake. Brownies keep cooking as they cool, so pull them out slightly underdone.
  • Wipe the knife between cuts for neat squares.
  • Store in an airtight box for 4 days. They taste even better the next day.
  • For a cakier brownie, add one more egg and 30 g more flour.
Why is my brownie top not shiny?

The eggs and sugar were not beaten long enough. Beat for a full minute after adding the eggs until the batter looks glossy.

Can I double the recipe?

Yes. Double every amount and use a 23 x 33 cm pan. Bake 5 to 8 minutes longer.
