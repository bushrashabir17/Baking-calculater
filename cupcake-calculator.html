How the cupcake calculator works

One pound of batter fills about 10 standard cupcake liners at 55 g each, or about 27 mini cupcakes at 20 g each. So 2 lb of batter gives about 20 cupcakes, and 5 lb gives about 50.

Every recipe here is written in baker's percentages. That means each ingredient is shown as a percentage of the flour weight, so the balance of flour, fat, sugar, eggs and liquid stays the same at every size. This is how professional bakers scale recipes.

Quick method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C and line the tin with liners.
  2. Make the batter as for a cake. Do not overmix after adding flour.
  3. Scoop into liners until two-thirds full.
  4. Bake 18 to 22 minutes, cool fully, then pipe on the frosting.

Amounts are approximate. Do a test batch before an order.

Common questions

How many cupcakes are in 1 lb of batter?

About 10 standard cupcakes (55 g batter each). A dozen cupcakes needs about 1.2 lb of batter.

How much frosting do I need?

The frosting amounts shown cover about the same number of cupcakes as the batter. It is enough for a generous swirl on each.

Why did my cupcakes dome and crack?

The oven was probably too hot or the liners were overfilled. Fill two-thirds and keep the oven at 180°C.

Can I bake these as a cake?

Yes. The batter is the same as our cake batter. See the cake calculator for pan sizes and times.
