Baking basics for beginners

  • Weigh ingredients in grams whenever you can. It is more accurate than cups.
  • Preheat the oven for at least 15 minutes before baking.
  • Use room-temperature eggs and butter for cakes and cookies.
  • Every oven is different. Start checking 5 minutes before the time in the recipe.
