Red Velvet Cake

Soft red layers with cream cheese frosting

Home Cakes Cupcakes Cookies Brownies Pizza & Pastry Recipes

About this recipe

Red velvet is a soft, tender cake with a very light cocoa flavor and a slight tang from buttermilk and vinegar. Its bright red color and white cream cheese frosting make it a favorite for birthdays and celebrations. This version uses oil, so the cake stays moist for days.

  • Serves: 12
  • Pans: two 20 to 23 cm (8 to 9 inch) rounds
  • Prep: 25 min
  • Bake: 28 to 35 min

Ingredients

For the cake

IngredientGrams / mlCups / spoons
All-purpose flour (maida)300 g2½ cups
Sugar300 g1½ cups
Unsweetened cocoa powder10 g2 tbsp
Baking soda5 g1 tsp
Salt6 g1 tsp
Vegetable oil360 ml1½ cups
Buttermilk (see note)240 ml1 cup
Eggs2 large2 large
Red food coloring (gel is best)15 to 30 ml1 to 2 tbsp
White vinegar5 ml1 tsp
Vanilla essence5 ml1 tsp

No buttermilk? Mix 240 ml milk with 1 tablespoon lemon juice or vinegar and wait 5 minutes.

For the cream cheese frosting

IngredientAmount
Cream cheese, cold, block style225 g
Butter, softened115 g (½ cup)
Icing sugar300 g (2½ cups)
Vanilla essence5 ml (1 tsp)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C. Grease two round pans and line the bottoms with baking paper.
  2. Whisk the flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda and salt in a large bowl.
  3. In another bowl, whisk the oil, buttermilk, eggs, food coloring, vinegar and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Pour the wet mixture into the dry mixture. Mix gently until just combined and no flour streaks remain. Do not overmix.
  5. Divide the batter between the pans.
  6. Bake 28 to 32 minutes for 23 cm pans, or 30 to 35 minutes for 20 cm pans, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then turn out and cool completely.
  8. Make the frosting: beat the butter until creamy. Add the cold cream cheese and beat just until smooth. Add the icing sugar and vanilla and mix on low, then on high for 30 seconds.
  9. Spread frosting between the layers, then over the top and sides. Chill 30 minutes before slicing.

Tips for success

  • Gel food coloring gives a bright red with less liquid. Liquid coloring can turn the cake brownish.
  • Frost only when the cake is completely cool.
  • Do not overbeat the cream cheese, or the frosting turns runny. If it is soft, chill it for 20 minutes.
  • Store covered in the fridge for up to 4 days. Let it sit out for 20 minutes before serving.
Why is my red velvet cake brown?

Too little coloring, liquid coloring, or too much cocoa. Use gel coloring and keep the cocoa to 2 tablespoons.

Can I make cupcakes?

Yes. Fill liners two-thirds full and bake at 175°C for 18 to 22 minutes. This recipe makes about 24 cupcakes.
