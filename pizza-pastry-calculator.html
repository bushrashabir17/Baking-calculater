How this calculator works

Pizza: the dough uses 62% water, 1% yeast, 2% salt and 3% oil (baker's percentages against the flour weight). One pound of dough makes about 1.7 pizzas of 30 cm (12 inch). Toppings are given for the same amount of dough.

Puff pastry: this is a quick "rough puff" dough with butter at 70% of the flour weight and water at 50%. One pound of dough makes about 9 pieces of 50 g each, such as patties, rolls, turnovers and twists.

Common questions

What is hydration in pizza dough?

It is the water weight compared to the flour weight. 62% hydration means 62 g of water for every 100 g of flour, which gives a soft dough that is still easy to handle.

Why must the butter be cold in puff pastry?

Cold butter stays in layers inside the dough. In a hot oven the butter melts and makes steam, which lifts the layers apart.

Can I use all-purpose flour for pizza?

Yes. Bread flour gives a chewier crust, but all-purpose flour also works well.

How do I stop patties from getting soggy?

Keep the filling dry and let it cool fully before filling the pastry. Wet or warm filling melts the butter layers.
