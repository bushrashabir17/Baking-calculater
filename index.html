How this calculator works

A "1 lb cake" is the size most bakeries sell: about 7 inches round, serving 6 to 8 people. This calculator uses a classic butter sponge recipe and scales every ingredient up or down for your size, so newcomers never have to guess.

Standard cupcakes use about 55 g of batter each, filling a liner two-thirds full. Mini cupcakes use about 20 g each.

Beginner baking tips

Why did my cake come out dense?

Usually from over-mixing after adding flour, old baking powder, or cold ingredients. Mix just until combined and use room-temperature eggs and butter.

Should I measure by cups or grams?

Grams are much more accurate. A cup of flour can vary by 30 g or more depending on how you scoop it. A cheap kitchen scale is the best first tool for a new baker.

How full should I fill the pan?

Fill cake pans about half to two-thirds full. Cupcake liners should be two-thirds full so they rise without overflowing.

How do I know the cake is done?

Insert a toothpick in the center. If it comes out clean or with a few dry crumbs, the cake is ready. Do not open the oven in the first 20 minutes.

How many people does a 1 lb cake serve?

Roughly 6 to 8 slices. A 2 lb cake serves about 12 to 16, and a 3 lb cake serves about 20 to 24.
