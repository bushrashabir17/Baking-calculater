Why the amounts are reliable

Every recipe here is written in baker's percentages. That means each ingredient is shown as a percentage of the flour weight, so the balance of flour, fat, sugar, eggs and liquid stays the same at every size. This is how professional bakers scale recipes.

  • Every size from 1 to 5 lb keeps the same ratios.
  • Grams are shown first because they are exact. Cups and spoons are close guides.
  • You also get pan size, oven temperature and time for the size you choose.

Amounts are approximate and ovens differ. Do a test bake before a big order.

Common questions

What does a 1 lb cake mean?

A cake of about one pound after baking, around 15 cm (6 inch) wide, serving 6 to 8 people. Bakeries price cakes by this weight.

Which size should I choose?

Use the number of guests. About 6 to 8 slices per pound for cakes, 10 cupcakes per pound of batter, and 15 cookies per pound of dough.
