How the cookie calculator works

One pound of dough is about 454 g and makes about 15 medium cookies of 30 g each. The recipes balance flour, butter and sugar so cookies spread just enough and stay chewy in the center.

Every recipe here is written in baker's percentages. That means each ingredient is shown as a percentage of the flour weight, so the balance of flour, fat, sugar, eggs and liquid stays the same at every size. This is how professional bakers scale recipes.

Quick method

  1. Beat the butter and sugars until creamy.
  2. Add the egg and vanilla. Mix in the dry ingredients until just combined.
  3. Fold in chips, nuts or fruit. Chill the dough for 30 minutes.
  4. Scoop 30 g balls, space them 5 cm apart and bake until the edges are golden.

Amounts are approximate. Ovens vary, so check the first tray early.

Common questions

How many cookies will I get from 1 lb of dough?

About 15 medium cookies if each ball weighs 30 g. Smaller balls give more cookies and need less baking time.

Why did my cookies spread too much?

The butter was too warm or the dough was not chilled. Chill the dough for 30 minutes and use cool, soft butter.

Why are my cookies hard?

Too much flour or too long in the oven. Weigh the flour and take them out while the centers still look slightly soft.

Can I freeze the dough?

Yes. Freeze shaped balls and bake straight from frozen, adding 1 to 2 minutes.
