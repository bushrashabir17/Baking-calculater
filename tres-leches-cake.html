Tres Leches Cake

Soft sponge soaked in three milks

Home Cakes Cupcakes Cookies Brownies Pizza & Pastry Recipes

About this recipe

Tres leches means "three milks". A light sponge cake is soaked in a mix of condensed milk, evaporated milk and regular milk, then topped with whipped cream. It stays soft and juicy because the sponge drinks up the milk. The cake must be made a few hours ahead, so it is perfect for parties.

  • Serves: 12
  • Pan: 23 x 33 cm (9 x 13 inch)
  • Prep: 30 min
  • Bake: 25 to 30 min
  • Chill: 3 hours or overnight

Ingredients

For the sponge

IngredientGrams / mlCups / spoons
All-purpose flour (maida)180 g1½ cups
Baking powder6 g1½ tsp
Salt1.5 g¼ tsp
Eggs, separated5 large5 large
Sugar (divided)200 g1 cup
Whole milk80 ml⅓ cup
Vanilla essence5 ml1 tsp

For the three-milk soak

IngredientAmount
Sweetened condensed milk1 tin (about 395 g)
Evaporated milk1 tin (about 410 g)
Whole milk or cream120 ml (½ cup)

For the topping

IngredientAmount
Whipping cream (cold)360 ml (1½ cups)
Sugar25 g (2 tbsp)
Vanilla essence5 ml (1 tsp)

Cups used: flour 120 g per cup, sugar 200 g per cup.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C. Grease a 23 x 33 cm pan and line the bottom with baking paper.
  2. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
  3. Beat the egg yolks with 150 g (¾ cup) of the sugar for 3 to 4 minutes until pale and thick. Stir in the milk and vanilla.
  4. Fold the flour mixture into the yolks until just combined.
  5. In a clean, dry bowl, beat the egg whites until foamy. Add the remaining 50 g (¼ cup) sugar and beat until stiff peaks form.
  6. Fold the whites into the batter in three parts. Be gentle so you keep the air in.
  7. Pour into the pan and bake 25 to 30 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
  8. Let the cake cool completely. Poke holes all over it with a fork.
  9. Mix the condensed milk, evaporated milk and regular milk. Pour it slowly over the cake, a little at a time, so it soaks in.
  10. Cover and chill for at least 3 hours, or overnight.
  11. Whip the cold cream with the sugar and vanilla until soft peaks form. Spread over the cake and serve cold.

Tips for success

  • Do not soak a warm cake. It turns mushy. Cool it fully first.
  • Keep egg whites free of any yolk or grease, or they will not whip.
  • If the milk pools at the bottom, give the cake more chilling time.
  • Keep it in the fridge. It stays good for 3 days.
Can I use a smaller pan?

Yes. For a half recipe, use a 20 cm (8 inch) square pan and divide every amount by two. Use our calculator to scale other cakes.

Can I make it without evaporated milk?

You can use full-cream milk instead, but the soak will be thinner and less rich. Add a little extra condensed milk for sweetness.
