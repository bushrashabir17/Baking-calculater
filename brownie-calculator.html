How the brownie calculator works

These are fudgy brownies made by melting butter and chocolate. Each pound of batter is about 454 g and makes about 12 squares. The batter should be about 3 cm deep in the pan, so the calculator gives the pan area you need.

Every recipe here is written in baker's percentages. That means each ingredient is shown as a percentage of the flour weight, so the balance of flour, fat, sugar, eggs and liquid stays the same at every size. This is how professional bakers scale recipes. Brownies have very little flour, so the percentages look high. That is normal for a fudgy brownie.

Quick method

  1. Melt the butter and chocolate together and let it cool a little.
  2. Stir in the sugar, then beat in the eggs and vanilla until glossy.
  3. Fold in the flour, cocoa and salt until just combined.
  4. Bake until a toothpick shows moist crumbs. Cool fully before cutting.

Amounts are approximate. Extras like nuts and chips add weight on top of the base batter.

Common questions

How do I make cakey brownies instead of fudgy?

Add one more egg and about 30 g more flour per pound of batter, and beat the eggs and sugar longer.

How do I get a shiny crackly top?

Beat the eggs and sugar for a full minute until the mix looks glossy before folding in the flour.

Why are my brownies dry?

They were probably overbaked. Brownies keep cooking in the hot pan, so take them out while a toothpick still shows moist crumbs.

How long do they keep?

In an airtight box, about 4 days. They taste even better on the second day.
