Pull Me Up Cake

Layered chocolate cake dessert in a tub

Home Cakes Cupcakes Cookies Brownies Pizza & Pastry Recipes

About this recipe

A pull me up cake is a dessert made in a tub or wide jar. Soft chocolate cake is cut into cubes and layered with a milk soak, chocolate ganache and whipped cream. You then scoop or "pull up" a spoonful with all the layers. It is a popular party and gifting dessert, and it uses simple parts you can prepare ahead.

  • Serves: 8 to 10
  • Container: 1.5 liter tub or 6 jars
  • Prep: 30 min (with a ready cake)
  • Chill: 4 hours

Ingredients

Cake base

One baked layer (20 to 23 cm) of our chocolate fudge cake, or any plain chocolate cake, about 400 g. Let it cool fully.

Milk soak

IngredientAmount
Milk200 ml (¾ cup plus 1 tbsp)
Sweetened condensed milk60 g (3 tbsp)

Chocolate ganache

IngredientAmount
Dark chocolate, chopped150 g
Fresh cream (hot)150 ml (⅔ cup)

Cream layer and topping

IngredientAmount
Whipping cream (cold)300 ml (1¼ cups)
Sugar or icing sugar30 g (2 tbsp)
Crushed biscuits (optional)50 g
Chopped nuts or chocolate shavingsfor the top

Method

  1. Make the ganache: pour the hot cream over the chopped chocolate, wait 2 minutes, then stir until smooth. Let it cool until thick but still spreadable.
  2. Stir the milk and condensed milk together for the soak.
  3. Whip the cold cream with the sugar until stiff peaks form.
  4. Cut the cake into 2 cm cubes.
  5. Layer 1: put a third of the cake cubes in the bottom of the tub. Sprinkle about a third of the milk soak over them.
  6. Layer 2: spread a third of the ganache, then a third of the whipped cream. Add a sprinkle of crushed biscuits.
  7. Repeat the layers two more times, finishing with whipped cream on top.
  8. Decorate with nuts, chocolate shavings or a drizzle of ganache.
  9. Cover and chill for at least 4 hours so the flavors blend. Serve cold.

Tips for success

  • Do not drown the cake. Sprinkle the soak evenly. The cubes should be moist but not mushy.
  • Use a clear tub or jars if you want the layers to show.
  • Whip the cream until stiff so it holds its shape between layers.
  • Keep chilled and eat within 3 days.
Can I make it in small jars?

Yes. Divide the layers between 6 jars. They are easy to serve and make good gifts. Keep them cold until you hand them out.

Can I use a different cake?

Yes. Vanilla sponge, brownies or a leftover chocolate cake all work. Brownies make it richer, so use less ganache.
