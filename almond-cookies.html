Almond Cookies

Buttery cookies with a nutty crunch

Home Cakes Cupcakes Cookies Brownies Pizza & Pastry Recipes

About this recipe

These almond cookies use ground almonds in the dough for a rich nutty flavor, with a whole almond pressed on top. They have crisp edges and a tender center. The dough comes together in one bowl and needs no special tools.

  • Makes: about 20 cookies
  • Prep: 20 min
  • Chill: 30 min
  • Bake: 12 to 14 min

Ingredients

IngredientGrams / mlCups / spoons
Butter, softened115 g½ cup
Sugar100 g½ cup
Egg1 large1 large
Vanilla essence5 ml1 tsp
Almond essence (optional)1 ml¼ tsp
All-purpose flour (maida)150 g1¼ cups
Ground almonds (almond flour)50 g½ cup
Baking powder2 g½ tsp
Salt1 ga pinch
Whole almonds for the tops2020

Cups used: flour 120 g per cup, sugar 200 g per cup, butter 227 g per cup. No almond flour? Grind blanched almonds in a blender until fine, but stop before they turn to paste.

Method

  1. Beat the butter and sugar for 2 to 3 minutes until light and creamy.
  2. Add the egg, vanilla and almond essence. Mix until combined.
  3. Add the flour, ground almonds, baking powder and salt. Mix until a soft dough forms.
  4. Cover and chill the dough for 30 minutes. This stops the cookies from spreading too much.
  5. Heat the oven to 170°C. Line a tray with baking paper.
  6. Roll the dough into balls about the size of a walnut (25 g each). Place them 4 cm apart.
  7. Press one almond into the center of each ball and flatten it slightly.
  8. Bake 12 to 14 minutes, until the edges are light golden.
  9. Cool on the tray for 5 minutes, then move to a rack. They crisp up as they cool.

Tips for success

  • Do not overbake. Pull them out when the edges are golden, even if the centers look soft.
  • Weigh the flour. Too much flour makes dry, crumbly cookies.
  • Store in an airtight tin for up to 5 days.
  • You can freeze the shaped dough balls for up to 1 month and bake them straight from the freezer, adding 1 to 2 minutes.
Why did my cookies spread flat?

The butter was probably too soft or the dough was not chilled. Chill the dough for the full 30 minutes.

Can I make them without eggs?

Replace the egg with 2 tablespoons of milk. The cookies will be slightly more crumbly.
